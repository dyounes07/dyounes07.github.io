01 / ABOUT

A little about me

My interest in computer science started in middle school through SciTech, where I got my first taste of programming with Scratch and LEGO robotics. That early curiosity stuck with me, and I began teaching myself web development and Python through Codecademy when I entered high school.

In Grade 10, I took both introductory and advanced high school computer science courses, including the Grade 12 course alongside older students. It was challenging, but it gave me an early opportunity to push myself technically and discover how much I enjoyed learning computer science.

I have always enjoyed the intersection of computer science and mathematics. Through enrichment and mentorship programs, I explored graph theory and cryptography. I later researched quadratic field cryptography with a PhD candidate as my mentor and presented our findings using LaTeX to professors, mentors, and other students.

Outside the classroom, I participated in coding and engineering clubs, worked as an executive with different teams, and joined my school’s rookie FTC robotics team. Working with the Java SDK to build and program a robot was one of the experiences that showed me how much I enjoy turning ideas into something tangible.

I’m now entering my second year as a Computer Science Specialist student at the University of Toronto, St. George. I’m especially interested in cybersecurity because it combines the things I enjoy most: understanding complex systems, thinking carefully about how they can fail, and designing better solutions.
